      How to play

      • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your home.
      • Rolling a 6 gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row and your turn is cancelled.
      • Land on an opponent's token to cut it — it goes back home and you get a bonus roll.
      • Tokens on star squares and coloured start squares are safe and can't be cut.
      • You need the exact number to reach the centre. Reaching home gives a bonus roll.
      • Each turn has a 20-second timer — if it runs out, a move is made for you.
      • First player to bring all 4 tokens home wins!
